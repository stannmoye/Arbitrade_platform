# Arbitrade design brief

## Theme Name
Midnight Ledger

## Intro
A calm, high-trust financial control room: deep midnight canvas, warm paper panels, and a single electric mint accent for actionable market movement.

## Probability
0.08

## Design movement
Editorial fintech dashboard with restrained neo-brutalist structure, soft depth, and disciplined data density.

## Core principles
Trust before excitement; hierarchy before decoration; every number carries a status; motion clarifies state; risk is visible without drama.

## Color philosophy
Ink navy and graphite create a quiet base. Warm ivory panels make dense data feel legible. Mint marks positive/active states, amber means review, and coral means blocked or negative. Avoid neon glow and casino-like gradients.

## Layout paradigm
A fixed left rail, wide primary canvas, top utility bar, and asymmetric grid of balance, opportunity, allocation, and activity cards. Mobile collapses into a bottom navigation and stacked cards.

## Signature elements
A compact compass-mark logo, opportunity cards with a live freshness bar, pill-shaped status tags, thin ruled separators, and a small “simulation mode” ribbon that never looks like production money.

## Interaction philosophy
Actions are explicit and reversible when possible. Sensitive actions wait for confirmation. Admin controls are visible but gated. Popups are concise, dismissible, and explain assumptions.

## Animation
Subtle 180–240 ms ease-out transitions, rising ticker dots, a gentle bar shimmer for fresh opportunities, and no forced motion. Respect reduced motion.

## Typography system
Use a modern sans serif for interface text and a slightly condensed display treatment for big figures. Preserve comfortable line height and tabular numerals for money.

## Brand essence
Quiet confidence for complex markets.

## Brand voice
Clear, precise, and honest. Say “estimated,” “simulated,” and “available capacity,” never “guaranteed.”

## Wordmark/logo
Arbitrade wordmark beside a simple compass/route mark: two offset chevrons meeting around a central point, suggesting price convergence and a controlled route.

## Signature brand color
Mint signal: #7CE7C2.

## Committed direction
Midnight Ledger is the single direction for this prototype. It applies to customer and admin screens.
